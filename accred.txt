Sample Letter for Accredited Investor

[Your CPA Firm's Letterhead]
[Date]
[Name of Investor or Entity]
[Investor Address]
[City, State, ZIP Code]

Subject: Accredited Investor Verification for [Investor Name] 

To Whom It May Concern:

This letter is to confirm that I, [Your Name], a Certified Public Accountant (CPA) licensed in the State of [Your State], have been engaged by [Investor Name] to verify their status as an "accredited investor" as defined in Rule 501 of Regulation D of the Securities Act of 1933. 
Based on our review of the financial documents provided by [Investor Name], including [list specific documents, e.g., tax returns for the past two years, bank statements, or brokerage statements], we have taken reasonable steps to verify that [Investor Name] meets the financial criteria. 
Based on this review, we certify that as of the date of this letter, [Investor Name] qualifies as an accredited investor by meeting the following criterion:
[Choose the applicable criterion and delete the other options]
Net Worth: The individual or joint net worth with a spouse exceeds $1,000,000, excluding the value of their primary residence.
Income: The individual has had an annual income of over $200,000 (or $300,000 with a spouse) for the past two years and has a reasonable expectation of reaching the same income level in the current year. 

This verification is for the purpose of demonstrating accredited investor status. The information provided is based on the financial documentation available at the time of this review. We make no assurance regarding the accuracy of information not specifically reviewed for this verification. 

Sincerely,
[Your Signature]
[Your Printed Name]
Certified Public Accountant
[Your State] CPA License #[Your License Number]
